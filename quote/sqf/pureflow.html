Staff Training Program

SQF Code requires that all personnel receive documented food safety training appropriate to their role — and that training records are available for auditor review. Jennifer reviews and documents all required training sessions as part of this engagement. Sessions are conducted on-site during scheduled visits, with supporting materials and signed attendance records provided to your internal team for the audit file.
Training deliverables include: session facilitation (on-site), signed attendance records, training topic summaries, and a completed training matrix for the audit file — ready for review by the certifying body auditor.
